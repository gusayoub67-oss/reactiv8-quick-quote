Roof Measurement
Simple measuring rule: Measure the main rectangular footprint wall-to-wall at ground level.

If part of the garage is blended into the main rectangle, it is already included. Do not add that part again.
Mixed-Pitch Roof
Estimate how much of the main house roof uses the second pitch. If the roof has more than two major pitches or is difficult to estimate, send it to Reactiv8 for review.
Garage / Addition
Measure Only the Part That Sticks Out
If part of the garage is blended into the main house and part sticks out, do not measure the whole garage.

Enter only the width of the projecting section and how far it extends past the main house wall.
Too complicated? If the garage/addition projects in more than one direction, has several roof sections, multiple pitches, or you are not confident in the measurement, do not guess. Send the property to Reactiv8 for review.
