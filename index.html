Roof Measurement
Simple measuring rule: Measure the main rectangular footprint of the house wall-to-wall at ground level.

If part of the garage is blended into that main rectangle, it is already included here. Do not add that part of the garage again.
Garage / Addition
Measure Only the Part That Sticks Out
If part of the garage is blended into the main house and part sticks out, do not measure the whole garage.

Enter only:
1. The width of the garage/addition where it projects from the house.
2. How far that section extends past the main house wall.

Example: If a 20-foot-wide garage sticks 10 feet past the front wall, enter 20 ft wide and 10 ft projection.
Too complicated? If the garage/addition projects in more than one direction, has several roof sections, multiple pitches, or you are not confident in the measurement, do not guess. Send the property to Reactiv8 for review.
